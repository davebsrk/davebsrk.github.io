Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

KidraPDF (“the app”, “we”) is a PDF reader and editor for Android. This policy explains what happens with your information when you use it. The short version: your documents never leave your device. We have no servers and no user accounts.

1. Your documents stay on your device

Opening, reading, editing, signing, merging, compressing, scanning and text recognition (OCR) all happen on your phone. We do not upload, collect or have access to your files.

2. What the app stores on your device

  • The list of recent and favourite documents, bookmarks and the last page you read.
  • Your signature, only if you choose “Keep it for next time”.
  • Your settings (for example, the theme).

This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Permissions

  • All files access (optional): used only to list the PDF files on your device so you can open them. The app reads and writes only the documents and images you work with.
  • Camera: the document scanner is provided by Google Play services and uses the camera only when you tap “Scan”. The app itself does not take or keep photos.
  • Internet: used to show ads and to process the optional “Remove ads” purchase.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads (see section 4).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form, and personalised ads are shown only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices for ads, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional “Remove ads” purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the app only receives confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Google account.

6. Scanner and text recognition (Google ML Kit)

Scanning and text recognition run on your device using Google ML Kit through Google Play services. We do not send your images or documents anywhere. Google Play services may download the required models and may collect limited usage and diagnostic information under Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data and we do not share your documents with anyone. The only third party involved is Google, as described in sections 4 to 6. When you use “Share”, you choose where your file goes.

8. Security

Password-protected PDFs stay protected when you edit them. Passwords you type are kept in memory only while the app is open and are never saved.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA) and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Your rights

Because we do not keep your personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side; everything is on your device and you can remove it by uninstalling the app. For advertising data, you can use your Android ad settings and Google’s tools at myadcenter.google.com. You can also contact us with any request.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.

Эта информация никогда не покидает ваше устройство и удаляется, когда вы удаляете приложение или очищаете его данные.
